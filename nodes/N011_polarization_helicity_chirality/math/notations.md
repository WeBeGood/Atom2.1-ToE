# N011 Notation + Conventions

## Propagation

- Worked examples use **k̂**=**ẑ**, k>0, and ω=ck>0.
- The complex phase is exp[i(kz−ωt)]. Real physical fields are real parts.

## Polarization basis

- Linear basis: **x̂**, **ŷ**.
- Circular/helicity basis: **e**σ=(**x̂**+iσ**ŷ**)/√2 for σ=±1.
- Norm: **e**σ*·**e**σ′=δσσ′.
- Helicity convention: i**k̂**×**e**σ=σ**e**σ.

## Rotation and viewing convention

At z=0, σ=+1 gives Re{**e**+e^{-iωt}}=(**x̂**cosωt+**ŷ**sinωt)/√2. It is counterclockwise when viewed from −z looking along +z. Reversing the viewing direction reverses the verbal clockwise/counterclockwise description.

## Naming rule

Use σ=±1 or the eigenvalue equation in derivations. If “left” or “right” is used, state both the phase convention and viewing direction.
