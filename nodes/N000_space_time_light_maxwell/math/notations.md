# N000 Notation + Conventions

## Spacetime and operators

- Flat Minkowski spacetime, inspected in one inertial Cartesian chart (x,y,z,t).
- Spatial metric δᵢⱼ and right-handed orientation x̂×ŷ=ẑ.
- ∇ acts only on spatial coordinates; ∂t denotes ∂/∂t.
- Repeated Cartesian indices are summed only where index notation is used explicitly.

## Maxwell sign conventions (SI)

- ∇·**E** = ρ/ε₀
- ∇·**B** = 0
- ∇×**E** = −∂t**B**
- ∇×**B** = μ₀**J** + μ₀ε₀∂t**E**

The vacuum propagation system sets ρ=0 and **J**=0 only after the source equations and units have been stated.

## Plane-wave phasor

- Real fields are the real parts of complex phasors with phase exp[i(**k**·**x**−ωt)].
- ω>0 and **k** points in the propagation direction.
- Faraday’s law then gives **B₀**=(**k**×**E₀**)/ω.
- A different Fourier phase convention changes intermediate signs and must be translated before comparing formulas.

## Symbols

- **E**: electric field; **B**: magnetic flux density.
- ρ: charge density; **J**: current density.
- ε₀: vacuum electric permittivity; μ₀: vacuum magnetic permeability.
- c: exact SI speed of light; Z₀=μ₀c: vacuum wave impedance.
- u: electromagnetic energy density; **S**: Poynting vector; **g**: field momentum density.
- δᵢⱼ: Kronecker delta; Tᵢⱼ: Maxwell stress tensor.
