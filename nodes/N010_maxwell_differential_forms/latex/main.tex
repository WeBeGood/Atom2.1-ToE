\section{Core Equations}
Let $F$ be the field-strength 2-form, $\mathcal H$ the excitation 2-form,
and $\mathcal J$ the charge-current 3-form. Then
\begin{align}
 dF &= 0,\\
 d\mathcal H &= \mathcal J.
\end{align}
The metric and material response enter through a constitutive relation between
$\mathcal H$ and $F$; the exterior equations themselves are metric-independent.

\section{Explicit 3+1 Split}
With $d=d_3+dt\wedge\partial_t$ and a right-handed spatial orientation, set
\begin{align}
 F&=B+E\wedge dt, &
 \mathcal H&=D-H\wedge dt, &
 \mathcal J&=\rho\,\mathrm{vol}_3-dt\wedge j.
\end{align}
Then
\begin{align}
 d_3B&=0, & \partial_tB+d_3E&=0,\\
 d_3D&=\rho\,\mathrm{vol}_3, & d_3H&=j+\partial_tD.
\end{align}
The vacuum relations $\mathbf D=\varepsilon_0\mathbf E$ and
$\mathbf H=\mathbf B/\mu_0$ recover the N000 vector equations in SI.
