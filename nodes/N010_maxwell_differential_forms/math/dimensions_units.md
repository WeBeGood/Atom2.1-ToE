# N010 Units + Dimensional Notes

Differential-form degree does not determine physical dimension. The components retain SI units, and a unit-aware formulation distinguishes field strength F from excitation ℋ.

## Spatial fields

- E: V m⁻¹ (spatial 1-form)
- B: Wb m⁻² = T (spatial 2-form)
- D: C m⁻² (spatial 2-form)
- H: A m⁻¹ (spatial 1-form)
- ρ: C m⁻³
- j: A m⁻² represented as a spatial 2-form after spatial dualization

## Vacuum constitutive law

In the inertial 3+1 split,

D=ε₀E,  H=B/μ₀.

Thus dℋ=𝒥 produces ∇·E=ρ/ε₀ and ∇×B=μ₀J+μ₀ε₀∂tE. Writing d⋆F=𝒥 without defining a rescaled current or the factors of μ₀ and c is not a complete SI statement.

## Potentials

The scalar potential has units V and the vector potential has units V s m⁻¹ = Wb m⁻¹. Components of a spacetime potential 1-form therefore depend on whether the time coordinate is t or ct; every component formula must state that choice.
