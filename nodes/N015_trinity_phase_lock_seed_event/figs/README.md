# Figures (N015)

## `two_wave_geometry.svg`

Deterministic vector schematic for the current baseline:

- **k₁** and **k₂** are separated by 120° and symmetric about +x.
- Their geometric sum points along +x and has magnitude k.
- The common electric polarization **ẑ** is perpendicular to the drawing plane.

The diagram is geometric, not evidence of particle creation. A future phase-observable plot should be generated from `code/sim.py`; its standard Maxwell extrema occur at local Γ=0 and π, not at 120°.
