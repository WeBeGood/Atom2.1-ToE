# N015 Notation + Conventions

## Fixed frame and phasor

- Right-handed Cartesian basis (**x̂**,**ŷ**,**ẑ**).
- Real fields are real parts of peak phasors with exp[i(**k**·**r**−ωt)].
- Both waves have ω>0, k=ω/c, and peak electric amplitude E₀.
- Common polarization **ê**=**ẑ**.

## Independent angles

- α=arccos(**k̂₁**·**k̂₂**) is the geometric crossing angle; α=2π/3 in this node.
- δ is the controllable constant phase offset of wave 2.
- Γ(**r**)=δ+(**k₂**−**k₁**)·**r** is the local relative phase.

Do not use one symbol for both the crossing angle and the phase offset.

## Symmetric wavevectors

- **k₁**=k(½,√3/2,0)
- **k₂**=k(½,−√3/2,0)
- **k₁**+**k₂**=k**x̂**
- **k₁**−**k₂**=√3k**ŷ**

## Observables

- u=(ε₀E²+B²/μ₀)/2: instantaneous energy density.
- **S**=(**E**×**B**)/μ₀: instantaneous Poynting vector.
- **g**=**S**/c²: vacuum momentum density.
- Angle brackets denote a time average over one optical cycle.
- I₀=ε₀cE₀²/2 is the cycle-averaged intensity of either input wave.

Polarization and phasor conventions inherit N011; energy/momentum conventions inherit N000.
