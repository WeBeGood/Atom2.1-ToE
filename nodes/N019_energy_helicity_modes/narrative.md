# N019 - Maxwell energy, magnetic helicity, effective line energy and C3 mass-mode audit

This node develops conditional mathematics and audits the Grok/Claude arguments supplied by Craig Fink / WeBeGood.

The complete derivations, assumptions, units and literature limits are in [math/proofs.md](math/proofs.md). The readable synthesis is [P004](../../papers/P004_cmb_trinity_audit/paper.md).

M3 denotes implemented numerical checks, not established particle physics. Physical identifications remain open. No parameter was adjusted to obtain a neutrino mass or cosmological constant.

Run `python nodes/N019_energy_helicity_modes/code/sim.py` from the repository root. Diagnostic JSON is illustrative and deterministic within ordinary floating-point tolerance.
