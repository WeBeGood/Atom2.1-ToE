# From a field representation to a physical seed line

A threshold condition S(E(X(s)),B(X(s))) >= S_threshold along a curve is a possible *definition*, not a proof of a persistent localized object. N015 has not supplied a demonstrated physical seed functional; no such result is assumed here.

N016's explicit standing field has zero net flux and is not localized. N019 supplies the honest thin-tube integral with transverse profile and geometry, and the conditional elastic model. The complete all-direction C3 representation is given in [CMB-03](cmb_trinity_proofs.md#cmb-03-a-precise-c3-transformation-of-the-whole-field).

A successful next step must define gauge-invariant seed observables, a finite energy (or controlled background-subtracted excess), boundary conditions and dynamics; then test persistence, perturbations, localization and total energy-momentum. An arbitrary threshold contour does not by itself establish a neutrino or a restoring phase force.
