# Explicit Beltrami-Maxwell candidate family

For real divergence-free e with curl e=k e, E=e cos(ckt), B=-e sin(ckt)/c is an exact standing Maxwell solution. One example is e=E0(cos kz,-sin kz,0). Rotated same-k sums remain eigenfields. This is a concrete completion of the earlier ansatz stub.

See [CMB-04 through CMB-06](cmb_trinity_proofs.md#cmb-04-exact-standing-beltrami-maxwell-solution) for Maxwell verification, electric/magnetic exchange, zero flux, arbitrary phase superpositions, and the finite-energy localization limitation. Broadband fields do not generally share a single curl eigenvalue.

The vector-potential ansatz curl A=kappa A remains a candidate gauge-dependent representation; use gauge-invariant E,B observables for physical claims and the conditions in N019 for magnetic helicity.
