## Quantitative audit retained for future work

The conditional energy-density result is 7/8 of the fringe-average reference for the stated equal-amplitude 120-degree geometry, polarization and local phase. The 1/8 contrast is not a demonstrated energy transfer. Three successive 1/8 losses of remaining energy give 169/512, not 3/8.

For the discussion's normalization H0=70 km/s/Mpc, H0/c is approximately 7.5671e-27 m^-1. Assuming kappa=1/8 and n_target=4.1e8 m^-3 requires sigma approximately 1.48e-34 m^2; a millimetre-scale wavelength-squared geometric area is about 28 orders of magnitude larger. These are conditional audit numbers, not measured interaction parameters. An independently derived suppression would be required.

Under independent Poisson encounter counts with mean mu and fixed fractional loss kappa, relative energy spread is sqrt(exp(mu*kappa^2)-1). At mean energy halving with kappa=1/8 this is approximately 0.30; a different coherent mechanism must explicitly establish its own spectral prediction.

Frequency conversion, forward momentum transfer, phase locking, reservoir heating/thermalization, and temporal signal stretching remain open. Zero net Poynting flux alone does not establish a zero-temperature reservoir. The receiving loop population and the observed CMB are not silently identified.

No scientific acceptance is implied by recording an entry. Promotion requires the corresponding interaction derivation, conservation accounting and testable predictions. No new animations or numerical alpha result are part of this ledger update.
